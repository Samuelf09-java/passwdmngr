Delete your account

Deletes the currently logged-in user's account,
logging them out, deleting their vault, and removing
them from accounts.bin. Requires confirmation (Y/n
prompt + password entry) for safety

Aliases:
  delaccount
  deleteaccount
  delacc
  rmacc

Flags:
  --force: Delete account directly; do not prompt for
    anything. USE AT YOUR OWN RISK
